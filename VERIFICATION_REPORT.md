## 4. Honest limitations

- Total-based floors (not per-module ratchets) — mechanical follow-up, tracked in the roadmap.
- The `fetchTransport` production path cannot be exercised in hermetic CI by law (ADR-0012); it is exercised through cassette/scripted transports at the same seam, and the endpoint map it carries is asserted by the C1 scanner.
- Breaker state is per-process by design (the failures themselves are journaled); multi-process sharing is deferred to the MS-5 daemon with an ADR.
